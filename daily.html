Ukraine looks to turn wartime digital experience into high-tech growth

Daily CET-6 Reading · adapted from Reuters reporting

Ukraine is seeking to leverage its experience in digital technology to expand high-tech exports in the coming years. Officials say the country has continued to provide online public services despite repeated attacks on infrastructure.

One important lesson has been the value of resilience. Data systems were designed with redundancy, while some infrastructure was moved underground. These measures helped services remain functional even when physical facilities were damaged.

Ukraine also hopes to apply innovations first developed for military purposes to civilian industries. Artificial intelligence, telecommunications and digital education are among the areas officials see as having strong potential.

The broader goal is to attract international investment and build partnerships with governments and institutions abroad. Supporters argue that rapid technological development could help transform parts of the economy, although turning wartime innovation into lasting commercial growth will require effective regulation and stable cooperation.
